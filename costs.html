🧠 Architectural Suitability for Kafka, EDB & Kagent AI

Choosing between these options depends on the lifecycle stage of your Kubernetes project and stateful performance requirements:

Nested k3s on Fly.io (Current)

Best For: Self-contained, lightweight PoC experiments and ephemeral preview environments.

Trade-offs: Runs K3s inside a Firecracker microVM. While cost-efficient ($25/mo), running heavy kernel I/O (Kafka page cache & PostgreSQL WAL) inside nested virtualization adds slight virtualization translation overhead under extreme benchmark saturation.

Lightweight Managed K8s (Civo)

Best For: Multi-node development clusters and cost-sensitive teams requiring true bare-metal/VM node separation.

Trade-offs: Lowest price point (~$21.73/mo) with zero control plane charges. Real independent worker nodes allow realistic testing of podAntiAffinity and multi-zone failovers without hyperscaler price overhead.

Google GKE Autopilot

Best For: Production promotion, strict compliance, enterprise multi-tenancy, and hyperscaler VPC peering.

Trade-offs: Fixed $73/mo cluster management charge makes single-pod PoCs expensive (~$100/mo). However, for production Kafka & EDB workloads, GKE provides guaranteed IOPS, NVMe SSD classes, and managed cluster security patches.
